*** \u001B[1m\u001B[4mRules for the classic version of Boggle:\u001B[0m ****

16 dice with letters on their faces are contained in a 4x4 grid. When the game session starts, the dice are shuffled and place randomly in that grid, then displayed to you.
The goal is to find and write down the maximum amount of words, the most lengthy possible, by forming strings of adjacent characters.
Once the timer has stopped, each player stops writing and counts their points. The player with the most points wins.
You only have a few minutes to do so, so keep an eye on the timer!

** \u001B[1m\u001B[4mThe specific rules\u001B[0m **
- Characters can be linked from the left, right, top, bottom, or diagonally.
- The words you find must at least have 3 letters.
- Any word, singular or plural and with any conjugation is allowed, as long as you can find it in the dictionary.
- For the languages that use accents: E can be considered as É, È, Ê, and so on...
- Proper nouns are not allowed.
- One letter cannot be used multiple times for the same word.
- Words inside other words are allowed (if a player finds "bought", they can also write "ought").
- Homonyms only count as 1 word.
- A same word found at different places in the grid cannot count multiple times.
- It is possible, if the players wish so, to consider that the letter "Q" also counts as "QU".
- A singular version of a word can be written in addition to its plural version, \u001B[1mIF\u001B[0m the player wrote down \u001B[1mBOTH\u001B[0m (the same goes for conjugation).
- \u001B[4m\u001B[31m/!\\u001B[0mIf multiple players have found the same word, points \u001B[1mARE NOT GIVEN\u001B[0m for this word!
